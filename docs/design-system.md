# Design System

## Direction

The homepage should feel like a serious industrial contractor, not a generic AI/SaaS landing page. The current reference priority is SteelMetriXX, with support from STR74, MetallPR, COMETAL, and Investsteel.

## Reference Patterns

- SteelMetriXX: white business header, high-contrast photo-led hero, large uppercase offer, orange vertical/linear markers, dense product strips, dark industry cards, full-width orange CTA bands.
- STR74: direct product-page structure with a clear H1, CTA, and short proof facts immediately under the offer.
- MetallPR: product/spec facts near the top, numbered production sections, practical engineering language.
- COMETAL: operational density, visible production/project media, category tiles, process cards, and proof through concrete workflow rather than decoration.
- Investsteel: straightforward full-cycle promise, production imagery, tables/specification blocks, and clear document/quote CTA.

## Stalkonstruktsiya Rules

- Use reference composition, not competitor claims, prices, photos, project counts, capacities, people, phone numbers, or addresses.
- Prioritize real industrial weight: steel-blue and gray surfaces, restrained typography, production/product planes, orange as a signal marker.
- Avoid abstract blueprint wallpaper as the dominant first impression. Blueprint details are allowed only as secondary technical texture.
- Avoid "proof cards" that look like SaaS feature cards. Use rows, strips, panels, tables, and spec blocks.
- Product/service blocks should read like catalog or contractor navigation, not marketing cards.
- Use full-bleed section backgrounds and a wide working container. Default desktop content max is 1440px; hero navigation strips may span the full viewport.
- Any missing production photo should be represented as a controlled visual slot, not as a fake case or fake facility image.
- Keep the approved copy constraints: no invented guarantees, no fake portfolio, no team/person sections, no unconfirmed production capacity.

## First Screen

- Header: white, businesslike, compact, with logo, navigation, contact status, and one calculation CTA.
- Hero: deep steel-blue/gray industrial plane; H1 is the primary object; orange marker beside the offer; one primary and one secondary CTA.
- Below hero copy: short verifiable facts in a horizontal specification row.
- Bottom of hero: product/service strip with 4-5 navigation tiles.
